CollectivSea: Ocean restoration through science, art, and experience.

CollectivSea is a SciArt collective, creating immersive experiences grounded in cutting edge marine science to help humanity somatically connect with the ocean, enabling a deeper understanding of how our ecosystems under the waterline are rapidly changing - ultimately we aim to inspire the emerging generation of blue activists.

Main mission

Create playful, accessible, sensory experiences that bring ocean science into public spaces, helping communities connect with marine ecosystems through wonder, sound, and story.

Expand blue-mind awareness by blending scientific research and creative practice so restoration is not only measured, but felt and shared.

Build bridges between artists, scientists, educators, and ocean advocates so people can discover, learn, and care for coastal habitats together.

Immersive science communication
Community-centered ocean literacy
Creative pathways to restoration action
